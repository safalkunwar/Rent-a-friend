# SATHI Help & Support Center

Last Updated: 2026-09-04

Welcome to the SATHI Support Center. SATHI is Nepal's premier Companion Guide and Local Experience platform. We are committed to helping you build trusted, safe, and memorable guided experiences in Nepal.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. What is SATHI?
SATHI connects local companions ("Sathis") with travelers and residents seeking local insights, language translation, or shared experiences in Nepal. Application approval reflects a recorded review, not a background check, professional certification, or guarantee of safety.

### 2. Is SATHI a dating application?
**No.** SATHI is intended for non-dating companionship and local experiences. Report inappropriate conduct; submitting a report does not guarantee an immediate response.

### 3. How do I book a Companion?
1. Find a companion on the **Explore** or **Search** page.
2. View their profile, reviews, and hourly rates (in NPR).
3. Click **Book Sathi** and enter your desired date, time, duration, and meeting point.
4. Review and send an unpaid booking request. Online payment initiation is unavailable.
5. Check your Bookings tab for acceptance. A saved or accepted booking is not proof of payment.

### 4. How does the messaging system work?
- Messaging uses participant permissions. Delivery, replies, and response times are not guaranteed; the app is not an emergency communication channel.

### 5. What is the SOS Safety Button?
The safety-alert form attempts to save one location reading and an alert record for authorized reviewers. It requires a registered account, location permission, and a successful database write. A saved record does not establish that anyone has seen it.

There is no continuous tracking, automatic contact notification, emergency dispatch, or monitored response. Contact local emergency services directly for urgent help; do not wait for this app.

### 6. How do I contact Support directly?
Use the in-app support form for non-urgent requests. SATHI has not published a verified staffed emergency helpline or response-time commitment. Previously displayed example phone numbers and office details must not be relied on.
